  title: "WorkStart"
  description: "A platform for finding jobs that require no experience, covering a wide range of industries in Argentina"
  project_img: "../../../../public/assets/workstart-live.webp"
  github_repo: "https://github.com/EliasPereyra/job-platform"
  live_link: "https://job-platform-two.vercel.app/"
  summary: "Landing a first job with no experience is hard, and many listings don't even say how much they pay. On WorkStart you search for jobs without signing up and, once you find one you like, you email the company directly, with no forms in between. Every opening has to state its province, work mode, and schedule before it can be published. Content is managed in Sanity, so publishing or editing a listing doesn't require touching code."
  key_features: 
    - "No sign-up needed to search for jobs or apply"
    - "Search jobs by title, province, and category"
    - "Browse openings by province from a map of Argentina"
    - "See all the jobs posted by a company"
    - "Optional account with email and password or passkeys to save favorite jobs"
    - "Responsive design"
  design_goals: 
    - title: "Publish listings without touching code"
      description: "Openings and companies are managed in Sanity Studio and queried with GROQ. With live content, whatever gets published shows up on the site without a redeploy."
    - title: "Apply without an account"
      description: "You don't need to sign up to apply: you email the company directly. The account is optional and is for saving favorites."
    - title: "Sign in without yet another password"
      description: "Accounts use Better Auth with email and password or passkeys, so you can sign in without remembering yet another password."
    - title: "User data kept apart from content"
      description: "Users, sessions, and favorites live in PostgreSQL on Neon, separate from the editorial content handled by Sanity."
    - title: "Tests on what people use most"
      description: "The search filters and the favorite button are tested the way a real user would use them, with Testing Library and user-event, and Vitest also covers the GROQ queries and the URL helpers."
---
